# موسوعة «قَالَ» التفاعلية | Master the Word Qāla in Quranic & Classical Arabic

A state-of-the-art, mobile-first Arabic learning web application dedicated to mastering all forms, grammar rules, phonetic mechanics, and Quranic rhetoric of the root **ق-و-ل** (Fi'l Ajwaf / Hollow Verb).

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. 📖 Complete Morphology (Sarf) Engine
- **14 Arabic Pronouns**: Full conjugations across:
  - Past Tense Active (*Māḍī*) & Passive (*Qīla*).
  - Present Tense Indicative (*Marfoo'*), Subjunctive (*Mansoob* with *Lan*), and Jussive (*Majzoom* with *Lam*).
  - Imperative (*Amr* - *Qul*, *Qūlā*, *Qūlū*, *Qūlī*, *Qulna*).
- **Ajwaf Weak-Letter Visualizer**: Step-by-step interactive demonstration explaining why the weak *Waw* transforms to *Alif* in `قَالَ`, and drops due to *Iltiqa' al-Sakinayn* (meeting of two silent consonants) in `قُلْتُ` and `قُلْ`.
- **Derived Forms (الأوزان المزيدة)**: Form II (`قَوَّلَ`), Form IV (`أَقَالَ`), Form V (`تَقَوَّلَ`), Form X (`اسْتَقَالَ`).
- **Nominals & Participles**: Active Participle (`قَائِل`), Passive Participle (`مَقُول`), and Masdars (`قَوْل`, `قِيل`, `مَقَالَة`, `أَقَاوِيل`).

### 2. 🎯 Final Vowel (I'rab) Gym
- **In-Depth Rationale for All 4 Endings**:
  - **Fatḥah (ـَ)**: *Mabni 'ala al-Fath*, Subjunctive after *Hatta/Lan*, *Maqool al-Qawl* clause as direct object.
  - **Ḍammah (ـُ)**: Default indicative present *Yaqūlu*, *Mabni 'ala al-Damm* with *Waw al-Jama'ah*, Subject (*Fa'il* / *Na'ib Fa'il*).
  - **Kasrah (ـِ)**: Phonological avoidance of two silent consonants (*Iltiqa' al-Sakinayn* in `قُلِ ادْعُوا اللَّهَ`), 2nd person feminine pronoun `قُلْتِ`.
  - **Sukūn (ـْ)**: Past with subject pronouns (`قُلْتُ`), Jussive (`لَمْ يَقُلْ`), Imperative (`قُلْ`).
- **Interactive Quiz Arena**: Immediate feedback, streak multipliers, and complete classical *I'rab* proofs.

### 3. 🕌 Quranic Corpus & Nouman Ali Khan Balaghah Gems
- 20+ deeply annotated Quranic verses.
- Morphological breakdown and color-coded syntactic tagging (Verb, Subject, Preposition, *Maqool al-Qawl*).
- **Bayyinah Balaghah Notes**: Explaining tense shifts (*Iltifat*), the rhetorical purpose of the passive `وَقِيلَ`, dialogue dynamics, and lexical choices.
- Web Speech API integration for Arabic audio recitation.

### 4. 🏗️ Sentence Synthesizer (Simple to Complex)
- 5 Progressive Tiers:
  - Tier 1: Simple Verb + Subject (`قَالَ زَيْدٌ`, `قَالَتْ مَرْيَمُ`).
  - Tier 2: Direct Speech Quotes (`قَالَ الْمُعَلِّمُ: الْعِلْمُ نُورٌ`).
  - Tier 3: Negated, Jussive & Subjunctive Constructions (`لَمْ يَقُلْ كَذِبًا`, `لَنْ نَقُولَ إِلَّا الْحَقَّ`).
  - Tier 4: Passive Voice + Deputy Subject (`قِيلَ لَهُمُ ادْخُلُوا الْجَنَّةَ`).
  - Tier 5: Multi-clause Quranic Structures (`وَإِذْ قَالَ رَبُّكَ لِلْمَلَائِكَةِ...`).
- Real-time syntax validation.

### 5. 🎮 Games Arena
- **Qāla Tetris (سقوط الكلمات)**: Falling blocks game matching pronouns with their corresponding verb conjugations and clearing horizontal lines. Features mobile touch D-Pad controls, audio effects, and level progression.
- **Harakah Blitz (برق الحركات)**: Rapid 45-second quiz testing the user's instinct for the last vowel with combo multiplier flames and review summary.
